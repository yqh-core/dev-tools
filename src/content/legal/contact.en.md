# Contact Us

## What you can contact us about

- Tool issues and bug reports
- Suggestions for new tools
- Accessibility problems (keyboard, screen reader, contrast, focus)
- Privacy and data questions
- Advertising and business inquiries

## What to include

Pick the template that matches your case and keep the subject prefix — it is how the mail gets sorted
and answered. Replace the `<placeholders>`; delete lines that do not apply.

### 1. A tool is wrong or broken

```text
Subject: [bug] <tool name> - <one-line symptom>

Page:              https://digdevbox.com/<tool>
What I entered:    <input, or describe the file>
What I expected:   <...>
What actually happened: <...>
Browser + version: <...>
Steps to repeat:   1) <...>  2) <...>
```

### 2. A tool that is missing

```text
Subject: [tool-request] <tool name>

Problem it solves: <...>
Who runs into it:  <...>
Inputs / outputs:  <...>
Tools I already tried here, and why they don't fit: <...>
```

### 3. Accessibility

```text
Subject: [a11y] <page> - <the barrier>

Assistive tech:    <screen reader / switch access / none — plus browser>
Barrier:           <keyboard trap | unreadable label | contrast | focus order | motion>
Where:             <page and the element>
What should happen instead: <...>
```

Screenshots and a short screen recording are welcome for all three, and never required.

## Response time

This site is maintained by an individual and we do not promise a response time. We try to read every email but cannot guarantee a reply to all.
