# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-19

## Overview

DigDevBox (digdevbox.com) is a collection of online tools. Most tools run entirely in your browser; the data you enter is not uploaded to our servers.

This page is written according to the services **actually deployed** on this site and is updated whenever that changes.

## How information is handled

### Data you type into tools

Encoding conversion, formatting, encryption, and text processing tools perform all computation locally in your browser. Your input is not transmitted or stored.

### Queries you submit

Two features need to send your input to this site's backend to complete a query:

- **WHOIS lookup**: the domain you enter
- **Website status check**: the URL you enter

Both are handled by interfaces deployed on Cloudflare Pages Functions and are used only to complete that query.

### Local storage

This site stores one interface preference (whether the tool guide is expanded) in your browser's localStorage. It stays on your device and is never uploaded.

This site does not use cookies to track your behavior.

### Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense. Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings.

### Hosting and network infrastructure

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Hosting and network infrastructure providers may process technical information required to serve requests, such as IP address, User-Agent, and request time, subject to those providers' own privacy policies.

## Services not currently deployed

As of the last updated date, this site does **not** deploy:

- Google Analytics
- Google Search Console site verification tags
- Google Fonts or other external CDN resources

This page will be updated if that changes.

## Your choices

- Clearing browser localStorage resets the local interface preference
- Manage cookies via your browser settings or Google Ads Settings

## Contact

For questions about this policy, you can reach us via the email on our [contact page](/contact).
